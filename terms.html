SubTracker Terms of Use

Last updated: 26 July 2026

SubTracker is provided for personal, non-commercial subscription tracking. Its optional Open Banking integration is read-only and may be used only with accounts owned by the user and explicitly linked to the user's restricted Enable Banking application.

SubTracker does not provide financial, legal, tax, or investment advice and does not initiate payments. Transaction matching and recurring-charge suggestions may be incomplete or inaccurate and must be reviewed by the user before application.

Availability depends on Enable Banking, participating banks, network access, and their applicable terms. The user is responsible for protecting device access, reviewing suggested changes, and revoking access when it is no longer required.
