SubTracker Privacy Notice

Last updated: 26 July 2026

SubTracker is a personal, non-commercial subscription tracking application. Bank access is optional, read-only, and intended solely for the application owner's own accounts.

Data handling

Imported bank transactions and subscription suggestions are stored locally on the user's device. SubTracker does not operate an account-data server and does not sell personal data.

When Open Banking is enabled, authorization and account-information requests are processed by Enable Banking and the selected bank under their respective privacy notices. The GitHub Pages callback host may process ordinary web request metadata and the one-time authorization callback URL under GitHub's privacy terms.

Control and deletion

The user explicitly chooses which accounts to synchronize and which subscription suggestions to apply. Open Banking access can be revoked and locally stored bank records can be deleted from the app's Bank Data settings.

Scope

No payment initiation functionality is provided. Bank credentials, PINs, passwords, and one-time codes are entered only on the bank's authorization service and are not collected by SubTracker.
